

# Project 01 — Commercial Retail Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on exploring commercial retail sales data
using Python, Pandas, and NumPy to identify meaningful business
insights and evaluate sales performance.

The project progresses from basic data exploration to
intermediate-level business analysis.

## Dataset

- File: `commercial_retail_sales.csv`
- Records: 40,000
- Columns: 17
- Period: 2024–2025
- Domain: Retail and E-commerce
- Source: Realistic synthetic commercial data

The dataset includes customer information, product categories,
sales channels, regional information, prices, costs, discounts,
and shipping details.

## Business Objectives

- Explore and understand sales data.
- Calculate revenue, profit, and profit margins.
- Evaluate product and category performance.
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
- Compare regional sales performance.
- Evaluate sales channels.
- Identify monthly sales trends.
- Perform descriptive statistical analysis.
- Communicate meaningful business findings.
